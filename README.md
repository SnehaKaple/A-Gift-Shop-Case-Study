# A Gift Shop Case Study (Excel Project)
Excel dashboard analyzing retail sales, customer behavior, and product trends with pivot tables &amp; charts.


##  Project Overview
This project demonstrates the use of **Microsoft Excel for business analytics and dashboard creation**.  
It analyzes sales transactions of a gift retail business to uncover revenue trends, customer insights, product performance, and delivery patterns.  
The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders make data-driven decisions for marketing, operations, and inventory planning.  



##  Objectives
- Build an **interactive dashboard** to visualize sales performance.  
- Track monthly revenue trends and seasonal fluctuations.  
- Identify top-selling products and high-value customers.  
- Analyze city-level sales and occasion-based performance.  
- Study the order-to-delivery timeline for logistics optimization.  



##  Tools & Techniques
- **Microsoft Excel**  
  - Pivot Tables  
  - Pivot Charts  
  - Slicers & Filters  
  - Conditional Formatting  
  - Data Cleaning & Transformation

##  Key Insights
- Sales peak during **festivals and special occasions** (Valentine’s Day, Raksha Bandhan, Diwali).  
- A fewproducts contribute significantly to total revenue.  
-Top 10 cities drive the majority of orders, indicating strong regional demand.  
- Larger orders tend to have **longer delivery times**, affecting customer experience.
